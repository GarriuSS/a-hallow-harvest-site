Privacy Policy

A Hallow Harvest · Sansi Studio · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

A Hallow Harvest does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game works fully offline.

What the game stores

Your save files and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks the full game is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The game only asks the App Store whether the purchase has been made on your account.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other device data.

Children

Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children.
