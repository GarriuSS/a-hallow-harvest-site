About the game

You wake among the hanged with no name, a sword, and a dog who seems to remember you.

A Hallow Harvest is a dark, hand-crafted pixel-art metroidvania made by a solo developer. Arel, a swordswoman in a yellow cloak, crosses a dying world whose grief is being harvested by a machine, and slowly learns who built it.

Every boss is built around a mechanic born from its story rather than just a health bar: seal the Warden with rune stones, catch the memories a grieving widow tries to swallow, strike a heart-hoarder on the beat of the true heart, and face a final enemy who mirrors the last move you made.

Features

  • 7 regions and 80+ hand-built rooms: the Cave of the Hanged, the Pallid City, the Undercity, the Harvest Gears, the Snowbound Pass, the Castle of Bones and the Crimson Ring.
  • 17 bosses, 6 of them hidden behind cracked walls, each with its own story mechanic.
  • Layered traversal: wall jumps, a grappling hand, a cloak glide that rides the wind, a shadow step through crimson veils and a crimson dive.
  • Tight combat with three-hit combos, parries, charged strikes and dodge rolls.
  • A skill tree, charms, a forge for your sword and memory jars that hold your life.
  • Memory chapters that piece together what Arel lost, and two endings decided by the player.
  • Touch controls designed for phones (adjustable size, opacity and layout) and full controller support.
  • 12 languages. Fully offline, no ads.
